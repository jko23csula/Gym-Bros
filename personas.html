Cal State LA Math Gym

Personas

Three key perspectives that guide how Gym Bros designs for better tutoring support, scheduling, and student success.

Persona 01

John

Role / Context

Math Gym Coordinator: John oversees tutoring services, supports the coaches, and uses attendance information to plan staffing and daily Math Gym operations.

One-Sentence Description

John needs a clear and reliable way to anticipate student demand so he can schedule enough coaches and maintain a supportive tutoring environment.

Goals

  • Forecast the busiest drop-in tutoring periods.
  • Maintain a balanced student-to-coach ratio during peak hours.
  • Keep tutoring support aligned with students' academic and classroom needs.

Frustrations

  • Unpredictable attendance makes staffing difficult.
  • Limited or unclear attendance data slows down scheduling decisions.
  • Overly complicated or inaccessible tools make information harder to use.

A Representative Quote

“Strong math skills are essential for students' futures, especially in a changing world shaped by new technologies and AI.”

What Success Looks Like

John can anticipate busy periods, schedule the right number of coaches, reduce overcrowding, and give students a more consistent tutoring experience.

Persona 02

Joseph

Role / Context

Math Gym Coach: Joseph works directly with drop-in students, explains math concepts, and manages several requests for help when demand is high.

One-Sentence Description

Joseph wants a manageable and organized tutoring environment where he can give every student focused, meaningful support.

Goals

  • Help students strengthen fundamental math skills and comprehension.
  • Make learning engaging while keeping it academically meaningful.
  • Give each student timely attention and clear guidance.

Frustrations

  • Too many students arriving at once makes individual support difficult.
  • An unstructured flow of students can make tutoring less efficient.
  • Inconsistent student attendance interrupts learning progress.

A Representative Quote

“What was once an impossible feat is now normal and evident.”

What Success Looks Like

Joseph has a balanced workload and enough time to help each student understand the material, build confidence, and leave prepared for class.

Persona 03

Robert

Role / Context

Math Student: Robert visits the Math Gym for help understanding course material, completing assignments, and preparing for quizzes and exams.

One-Sentence Description

Robert wants timely and approachable math support that helps him understand difficult concepts and stay confident in his academic progress.

Goals

  • Understand difficult math concepts and complete assignments successfully.
  • Build confidence, stronger study habits, and self-discipline.
  • Stay on track for graduation and future career opportunities.

Frustrations

  • Long waits during busy tutoring hours delay the help he needs.
  • Inconsistent attendance can cause him to fall behind.
  • Managing time and asking for help can be difficult when coursework becomes overwhelming.

A Representative Quote

“Live, laugh, and succeed with real confidence.”

What Success Looks Like

Robert receives help without a long wait, understands the material, completes his courses successfully, and moves toward graduation with greater confidence.
