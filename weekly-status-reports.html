Project Goal

Our goal is to help the Math Gym improve the student experience by forecasting drop-in tutoring demand and supporting a more balanced student-to-math-coach ratio. We will know we have succeeded when coaches feel less overwhelmed during peak hours and students receive timely support, leading to stronger participation and retention. The solution is also intended to be adaptable for other tutoring centers facing similar scheduling and staffing challenges.

Executive Summary

The project is on track and currently ahead of schedule. The functional prototype has already been deployed to the server and can process general data, positioning the team to complete the upcoming sprint work on time.

Team Reflection

We learned that the client is extremely satisfied with the prototype and wants us to verify the accuracy of the remaining Fall data as it is uploaded. One possible improvement is to add a checker that identifies overlapping records with the same check-in and check-out times on the same date. We could also present the prototype to Math Gym tutors and other users to gather additional feedback before the next development stage.
