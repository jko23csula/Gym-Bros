02

Shared Purpose

The project vision and definition of success

Goal

The shared goal of this project is to create an intuitive dashboard for the Cal State LA Math Gym. The dashboard will transform data currently stored in Excel files into clear, meaningful visualizations and analytics. This will reduce the time and effort required to interpret large amounts of information, allowing the user to focus on identifying trends, demonstrating the Math Gym's impact, and presenting compelling evidence to current and potential funders.

What success looks like

The system should allow users to easily track student participation, identify trends, measure program outcomes, and generate evidence for funding proposals. The overall experience should feel simple, efficient, and not overwhelming, giving the Math Gym team more time to focus on student support, program improvement, and long-term growth.

Decision-Making

A fair path from discussion to action

How decisions are made

The team will make decisions based on majority votes.

How disagreements are handled

Each member should have the opportunity to explain their perspective and provide supporting evidence on that opinion. Majority vote should allow us to move forward with the decision and all team members should support it.

05

Working Agreements

Participation, accountability, and deadlines

Participation

Active participation looks like everyone is on the same page and opening communicative about the project.

Accountability

We will have a checklist every week of what each individual needs to do. That way when a deadline is missed by a group member, the rest of us can hold that group member accountable.

Deadlines

We will have a calendar that will list the deadlines throughout the course.

06

Conflict Resolution

How concerns are addressed constructively

Unresponsive teammate

Reach out to the group member and see how they are doing. If they aren't responsive then we will bring it up to the professor.

How concerns are raised

The safest way for one of us to raise the concerns is to mention it when we meet with our groups.

When to involve the instructor

When we notice that it is beyond our control.

Success Metrics

What the team aims to deliver and demonstrate

What will make us proud

Being able to deliver a product to our consumer that they will use throughout the course duration.

Behaviors that define success

When each group member can happily and confidently describe the process and the outcome of the overall experience of the product.
