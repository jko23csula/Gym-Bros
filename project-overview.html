Project Goal

What we aim to improve and how success will be measured

Our project will help the Math Gym improve the student experience by forecasting drop-in tutoring demand and supporting a more balanced student-to-math-coach ratio. We will know we have succeeded when coaches feel less overwhelmed during peak hours and students receive timely support, leading to stronger participation and retention. The solution is also intended to be adaptable for other tutoring centers facing similar scheduling and staffing challenges.

04

Out of Scope

What this project will not include

  • We will not measure individual student performance or day-to-day academic improvement.
  • We will not collect students' personal backgrounds or their reasons for joining the Math Gym.
  • Unlike some other projects, we will not build a website for the client. Our focus is forecasting demand and supporting better staffing decisions.

Planned Agenda for Prototype Showing

A structured plan for presenting the prototype and gathering useful reactions from the client.

  1. Step 1 · Roles

    Justin will introduce the team, the client, and the purpose of the meeting. One teammate will lead the prototype demonstration, while another teammate will capture the client's reactions, questions, and recommended changes in the meeting log.

  2. Step 2 · Set the Table

    We will explain that this is an early prototype of the Math Gym demand-forecasting dashboard. The layout, sample data, filters, and predictions are still being developed, so some parts are intentionally rough. We want to learn whether the dashboard presents the most useful information clearly.

  3. Step 3 · Goal of the Meeting

    Our goal for the next 30 minutes is to hear honest reactions—not to ask for approval. We want to understand what is useful, confusing, missing, or should be changed before we continue developing the prototype.

  4. Step 4 · Project Goal

    Our project will help the Math Gym forecast drop-in demand and better balance the number of students with available math coaches so students receive timely support and coaches are less overwhelmed during peak hours.

  5. Step 5 · What We Will Demo
    1. Dashboard overview: Show the key demand, attendance, and staffing information, then pause for reactions.
    2. Demand forecast: Show how the dashboard identifies expected busy days, times, and subjects, then pause for reactions.
    3. Filters: Demonstrate how users can filter information by date, time, course, or subject.
    4. Staffing comparison: Compare predicted student demand with available math coaches, then pause for reactions.
    5. Data upload and validation: Explain how attendance or scheduling data may be uploaded, checked, and analyzed.
    6. Summary and decisions: Show how the information could support staffing, funding, and subject-offering decisions, then pause for final reactions and questions.

Prototype Change Log

Feedback received during the prototype review and the team's decision for each requested change.

What They Asked Decision Why We Made This Decision
Allow users to filter the dashboard by week. Approved — We are building it This will show attendance for each week and help determine tutoring needs during the weeks before, during, and after exams.
Allow custom filtering by date range. Approved — We are building it This will show how much math support was needed during a specific period rather than only by week. It can also help determine when both staff members are needed.
Create individual usernames. Approved — We are building it Individual accounts will keep each person's data queries and filters separate when multiple users access the dashboard at the same time.
Create a filter for different semesters. Approved — We are building it This will allow the Math Gym to compare attendance from previous semesters with the current semester, especially as students who previously used library tutoring are redirected to the Math Gym.
Provide a dedicated departmental server for storing the data. Tabled — Good idea, not now A separate server could improve convenience and limit data access, but it would require additional departmental approval, technical work, and an HTTPS certificate.
